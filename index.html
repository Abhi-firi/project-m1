How I think

I don’t start with the solution. I start with the problem.

A startup rarely hands you a perfectly defined task. My approach is to create clarity before creating activity.

Understand→Diagnose→Research / Learn→Hypothesise→Prioritise→Execute→Measure→Iterate

Example: “We have leads, but too few become customers.”

I’d first understand the funnel and where the drop happens. Then I’d inspect lead quality, ICP fit, messaging, qualification, sales conversations, objections, follow-up and handoffs before choosing an intervention.

The output isn’t a presentation.

The goal is a useful next step: a tested hypothesis, a system, a process, an experiment, an automation or a decision the team can actually act on.
